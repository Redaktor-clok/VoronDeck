Modifications for VoronDeck, 2026:
- Renamed family and PostScript names to VoronDeck Orbit / VoronDeckOrbit-*.
- Added 66 Russian Cyrillic mappings: 47 explicitly drawn letter outlines,
  19 equivalent Latin forms copied into separate Cyrillic glyph records.
- Added drawn UI symbols middot, numero and approximately-equal.
- Added separate Cyrillic GPOS kerning, preserving the original Latin tables.
- Instantiated the original variable face at static weights 400 and 600.

Generator: design/fonts/vorondeck-orbit/build_font.py in the workspace.
No Noto or other font supplies the Russian Cyrillic outlines.
The historic orbitron_* bitmap filenames and C symbols are internal identifiers,
not the family name of the modified fonts. Modified font software remains OFL-1.1.
